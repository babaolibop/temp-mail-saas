# Logo specification

Deliver a scalable SVG master for the coordinated lockup and standalone mark, with light, dark, and monochrome variants. Keep the vector silhouette recognizable without effects and optically center the mark with the wordmark. Supply favicon SVG and raster fallback variants as required by supported browsers.

| Proposed use | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Standalone brandmark | Minimum 24 CSS px outside favicons |
| Full lockup | Minimum 120 CSS px wide, subject to legibility review |
| Clear space | At least one quarter of the mark height on each side |
| Favicon | Review simplified artwork at 16 and 32 px |
| Accessible labeling | Use product text once per link. Hide decorative artwork when the link already has its accessible name. |

Test the silhouette at minimum sizes, monochrome reproduction, and real light/dark surfaces. Approve final spacing and sizes after optical review. Preserve proportions, permitted contrast, and alignment in shipped variants. Record asset names and usage limits after approval.
