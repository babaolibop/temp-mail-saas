# Brand identity

Use Temp Mail SaaS as the current product name. The tone is direct, calm, and privacy-aware. Explain expiration and limitations in plain English. Use typography, purposeful spacing, Zinc surfaces, and controlled Indigo consistently across the shell, forms, and installer.

Commission an original custom brandmark and coordinated wordmark. Explore several silhouettes and evaluate recognizability, optical balance, small-scale legibility, and distinctiveness. An envelope or stock icon requires explicit design review before it can qualify as a final mark. Document candidate rationale and approved variants. Keep reference products as UX references.

Review applications on light and dark navigation, favicon, compact viewport, and monochrome surfaces. After approval, register the master files and permitted variants under the [logo specification](logo-specification.md). No logo assets are defined by this documentation phase.
